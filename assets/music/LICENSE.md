## Selection record / 選定時の比較

The official recordings for Cut and Run, Hitman (USUAN1300013), Volatile Reaction, Five Armies (USUAN1100875), and Rocket Power were obtained and decoded. Metadata, waveforms/levels and short excerpts were compared. Cut and Run and Hitman were provided as audition previews, and the user selected Cut and Run. Volatile Reaction supplies an orchestral tension phrase; Rocket Power supplies a faster-feeling rhythmic funk phrase. Five Armies was the other orchestral candidate.

The agent cannot make a human listening judgment in this environment. It verified decoded audio, timing, browser playback and mix control; the selection is not a claim that an audio engineer listened to and approved the final mix. Final loudspeaker/headphone evaluation remains a human check.

Checked: 2026-11-21. Credits and the license link also appear in the game's settings screen.
